How I work

Four habits behind the results

Discovery before build

Validate the problem with real users and real market data before committing a team to it. Product strategy is only as good as the evidence underneath it.

AI where it moves a metric

I've shipped AI and ML in healthcare for over a decade: patient matching, risk prediction, payer-aware ordering, LLM-written appeals. Each one was tied to accuracy, revenue or hours saved.

Compliance is part of the product

HIPAA, SOC 2, HITRUST and ISO are designed in from day one, which lets health systems and payers say yes faster.

Lean, then scale

Ship a working product, learn from it, then invest. A self-service platform cut implementation time by about two months, and offshore transitions cut development cost by 60%.
