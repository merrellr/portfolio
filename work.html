Product Advisory · 2025–26 · Built by me

AI skills assessment & learning platform

+

Challenge

Product managers are being asked to adopt AI quickly, but a single course library doesn't fit everyone. Some PMs have never used it, while others are already advanced practitioners, and both groups waste time on content pitched at the wrong level.

Approach

Lucem Health · AI · Patient journeys

Patient care pathways before and after diagnosis

Longitudinal modeling that shows how patients actually move through the health system.

60%patient engagement, 2× industry benchmark
+

Challenge

Health systems couldn't see which steps on the way to a diagnosis were related to it and which were noise, or whether patients stayed on evidence-based care afterwards.

Approach

  • Analyzed encounters, symptoms, labs, imaging, medications and notes over time to find early warning signals and typical trajectories.
  • Separated pathways related to the disease or device from unrelated care.
  • After diagnosis, tracked adherence, follow-ups, lab and imaging milestones, and treatment response.

Outcome

Vynca · Clinician & patient experience

E-signature that works in real clinics

Live and asynchronous signing for patients, witnesses and physicians.

73%digital ACP completion rate
+

Challenge

Most clinic laptops don't have touch screens, and not every signer is ready during the visit. Health systems also wanted as few physician logins as possible.

Approach

  • Synchronous: sign on a tablet or phone during an in-person or virtual visit.
  • Asynchronous: scheduled email or text notifications when each signer's step is ready (patient, up to three witnesses, physician), with 2FA requirements checked against what each customer needs.
  • A document upload path for the 10 to 20% of workflows that stayed on paper, with quality checks.

Outcome

Contributed to a 73% digital completion rate for patient-centered ACP workflows, a leading result in the space. A self-service platform cut average implementation time by about two months.
